## C1. Retention with the nominal gap held fixed

**Pool.** TS0's MFE pairs with p >= 0.99 at lambda = 1, from registration 2's records as
`scripts/certfold_checks.py:base_records` builds them, the code path the earlier additions
used. "Certified" is registration 1's label under the envelope at lambda = 1; retention is
`ret_primary`, retention under the three whole tables.

**Gap.** The pair's nominal gap X0(q) - E0(s*), from registration 1's run records
(`nominal_gap_kcal`), joined on (sequence index, pair).

**Statistic.** R4's (`certfold_reg2._r4_spec("ret_primary")`): mean retention of certified pairs
minus mean retention of uncertified ones within each stratum, pooled weighted by the stratum's
pair count.

**Strata.**
- **Primary: deciles of the nominal gap.** Ties stay in one bin: a pair's decile is
  floor(10 k / N), where k is the number of pool pairs with a strictly smaller gap and N the
  pool's size.
- **Secondary: TR0 length stratum crossed with gap tercile.** Length strata as in R4 (strata 0
  and 1 merged); terciles over the whole pool, floor(3 k / N).

**Units.** Within a stratum, each sequence's pairs in that stratum form one unit. Units are
resampled with replacement within their stratum, by
`certfold_exploratory.stratified_bootstrap`: 10,000 resamples, seed tag 95, with no strata
merged.

**Drop rule.** A stratum is dropped, on the full data and before any resample, when its certified
or its uncertified pairs number fewer than 20. The number of strata and of pairs dropped is
reported.

**Reading, fixed now.**
- If the primary pooled interval lies above 0: "certified pairs keep a retention advantage when
  the gap is held fixed".
- Otherwise: "the advantage is not shown once the gap is held fixed".
- The secondary is reported in S10 only, with no reading.

**Placement.** One sentence in Section 6's stability paragraph, with matching wording in
contribution 1 and the conclusion. The table goes in S10.

**Fallback.** No text change beyond D4.

## C2. What Type B is made of

**Matrix.** M is the count matrix of TR0+TS0's 11,955 distinct pair-bearing references on their
4,561 used columns, built by `turner_typeb.compute_basis`, the code path of the 426 directions.
Type A restricted to used columns, A_r, is spanned by N1 and N2 restricted to those columns.
Type B is the orthogonal complement of A_r within the null space on used columns.

**Groups.** Columns of M that are identical integer vectors form a group. D is the span of
e_a - e_b over pairs of columns in the same group. So dim D is the sum over groups of
(size - 1), and D lies in the null space.

**Exact dimensions,** with no floating point:
- **dim(D ∩ A_r)** is 2 minus the rank of D's defining constraints evaluated on N1 and N2. A
  vector lies in D exactly when it is zero on columns in no group and sums to zero within every
  group.
- **dim(D ∩ Type B)** is dim D minus the rank of the 2 x dim D matrix of inner products of N1
  and N2 with D's basis. Within the null space, Type B is the part orthogonal to A_r.
- Both ranks are computed exactly over the rationals and modulo the two primes of Algorithm S1,
  2,147,483,647 and 1,000,000,007. All three must agree.

**Reported.**
- the number of groups with two or more members;
- dim D, dim(D ∩ Type B) and dim(D ∩ A_r);
- the split of D ∩ Type B across tables: the share of the trace of its orthogonal projector on
  each table's entries, computed in floating point and descriptive.

**TR0 alone.** All of this is repeated on TR0's references and TR0's 448-direction basis.

**Checks.** D ⊂ null(M) exactly (M (e_a - e_b) = 0 in integers), and both dimensions are at most
those of their spaces (2, and 426 or 448).

**Reading.** Descriptive only, with no threshold.

**Placement.** One sentence in Section 3's Type B paragraph, "(post hoc) N of the 426 directions
are differences of entries read by exactly the same reference structures", with details in S1.

## C3. Special hairpins on

**Configuration.** ViennaRNA 2.7.2's pinned model with dangles 0 and special hairpins on, every
other field as pinned, read back on every compound. It is set the way
`scripts/certfold_default_cert.py` sets its model (registration 1's functions, the model
rebound in the script's own processes, every fold's E0 checked against the count model at
Extractor(special, 0, 1)). The set is registration 1's: B_four, the envelope E_1 at lambda = 1,
and its weights and callback.

**Steps, in order.** Each must pass before the next; a failure stops C3.
1. **Type A.** From the generating set at this configuration (Algorithm S1's rows, exact rank
   modulo both primes, `turner_nullspace.analyse_config`): its dimension, the analytic
   directions' null check on every generating row, and the basis described in words.
2. **Invisibility.** TR0's references' counts at this configuration against B_four: max |M B|
   on the four tables' columns, beside the same quantity at the registered configuration. The
   four-table counts must be identical at both settings for every reference.
3. **Gate.** G1 (the callback identity, both backends, registration 1's 20 sequences and
   constructed rows), then G2 on registration 1's 200 sequences with the halved-weights power
   check, at the ball radii 1, 5 and 20 and the envelope. A false certificate stops C3. Nothing
   is tuned.
4. **Labels.** TS0's 1,288 sequences, labels at lambda = 1 under E_1, with no radii and no
   accuracy. s* is this configuration's MFE structure, and every fold is checked as in
   `certfold_default_cert` (the recursion-side identity, E0 against the count model, no
   admissible pair missing from the Zuker set). Reported: the certified-present share of MFE
   pairs, pooled and by TR0 length stratum, beside registration 1's 40.9%; and the number of TS0
   MFE structures that differ from registration 1's s*.

**Reading.** Descriptive, post hoc.

**Placement.** S7, beside the default-model paragraph, plus one clause in the Limitations
sentence on configuration.

**Fallback.** In Limitations, the documented reason special hairpins are off, or, if none is
documented, that it was the configuration pinned before the study. Then: the theory applies
unchanged, because the set moves only interior-loop entries, but no labels were computed there.
